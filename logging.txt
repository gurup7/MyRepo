On the target OpenShift platform, iTicket will leverage enterprise observability services to provide end-to-end visibility across all three deployable units. AppDynamics will serve as the Application Performance Monitoring (APM) solution, with the AppDynamics agent embedded in the Liberty container to capture real-time transaction traces, service call maps, response-time baselines, and JVM health metrics — enabling rapid root-cause analysis for performance degradations or failures across the Spring controllers, Hibernate data layer, and IBM MQ messaging paths. Kibana, backed by the EFK (Elasticsearch-Fluentd-Kibana) stack integrated into the ECS platform, will provide centralized log aggregation and search — replacing the current server-local std_out and std_err log files that Autosys writes to disk, with structured, queryable logs collected automatically from all pod stdout/stderr streams across the Liberty backend, Apache HTTP frontend, and batch job containers. Additionally, Prometheus and AlertManager will handle infrastructure-level metrics (CPU, memory, pod restarts, HPA scaling events) and alert routing — replacing Autosys's alarm_if_fail mechanism with configurable alert rules that notify operations via PagerDuty or the team's incident management channel. Together, these tools ensure that application health, business transaction flow, and batch job execution are observable from a single pane of glass, without requiring SSH access to individual servers as in the current VM-based topology.
