2. Critical Issue: T3S Protocol Limitation

The Core Problem:

    Liberty does not natively support T3S protocol (WebLogic proprietary RMI protocol)
    T3S is implemented entirely within Oracle WebLogic runtime and client JARs
    IBM has no license to implement Oracle's proprietary wire protocol

Bidirectional Connectivity Concern:

    External systems (E-Blotter, E-Dealer) are also WebLogic-based but operate outside the EDT domain
    Current setup uses T3S for bidirectional communication (both sending and receiving messages)
    If external systems have EDT queues defined as "foreign queues" on their side, they cannot connect via T3S to Liberty
